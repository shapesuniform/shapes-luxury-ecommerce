Refund & Exchange Policy (Exchange Only on Defects)

Due to the bespoke, made-to-order artisanal nature of our bridal couture and structured corsetry, all sales are final. We do not offer cash or monetary refunds under any circumstances.

No Cash Refunds
Exchange Only on Verified Defects

Eligibility for Defect Exchange:

  • Manufacturing Defects & Transit Damage: An exchange is provided strictly and solely in the rare event that the garment has arrived with a verifiable manufacturing defect, torn fabric, broken structural boning, or physical transit damage.
  • Reporting Timeframe (48 Hours): You must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery by emailing us at shapesuniform@gmail.com or messaging our official concierge on WhatsApp at +91 98333 92756 with your Order Reference Number (e.g. #SH-0001).
  • Unboxing Video / Photo Proof Required: Clear photographs and/or an unboxing video displaying the specific defect and original packaging tags are mandatory for inspection by our quality assurance team.
  • Exchange Fulfilment: Upon verification and approval, our team will arrange a complimentary pickup. We will repair the defect or craft a brand-new replacement piece and deliver it to you at zero additional cost.
  • Non-Eligible Cases: Exchanges or returns are not accepted for change of mind, personal preference, or minor natural handloom slub variations. Fit alterations can be scheduled complimentary at our Chembur, Mumbai flagship boutique.
