100% Made-To-Order Haute Couture

At Shapes By Satiinder Kaur, every single creation is custom handcrafted exclusively to client order. Because artisan allocation, handloom silk cutting, and metalwire embroidery drafting commence immediately upon transaction authorization, orders cannot be cancelled or modified once placed.

1. Immediate Production Allocation

Once your order payment is confirmed through Razorpay, raw materials (mulberry silk, pure linen, zardozi dabka) are specifically cut and allocated to your piece by our master craftsmen. Therefore, all sales are final upon checkout.

2. Order Review & Size Verification

  • We strongly recommend double-checking your chosen size, color, and delivery address before completing your payment.
  • Within 24 hours of placing an order, our concierge team contacts you directly to confirm exact bust, waist, and hip measurements to guarantee an impeccable fit before tailoring begins.

3. Failed or Duplicate Transactions

In the event that an amount is debited from your bank account due to a technical glitch during Razorpay checkout but the order status fails on the website, Razorpay's automated banking reconciliation system will reverse the full transaction back to your original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.
