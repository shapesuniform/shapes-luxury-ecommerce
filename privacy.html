Privacy & Data Protection Policy

At Shapes By Satiinder Kaur (operated by SHAPES UNIFORM), we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our esteemed patrons. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect your information in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and RBI digital commerce guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact, and email address.
  • Delivery & Fitting Data: Complete shipping address with postal pin code, and bespoke garment sizing measurements (bust, waist, hip, trouser length).
  • Transaction Records: Unique Order Reference Numbers (e.g. #SH-0001) and Razorpay transaction IDs.

2. How We Protect Payment Information

All financial transactions are conducted directly through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant 256-bit SSL encrypted infrastructure. We never view, collect, or store your credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or NetBanking credentials on our systems.

3. Use of Personal Information

Your personal data is used solely for the following business purposes:

  • Allocating artisan resources and crafting your bespoke garment.
  • Re-verifying precise fitting measurements prior to cutting fabric.
  • Dispatching packages via insured national courier partners and sending live tracking alerts.
  • Responding to customer service inquiries and order updates.

4. No Third-Party Selling or Marketing Spam

We maintain a strict zero-spam policy. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention & Security

Client records are stored securely on Google Firebase Cloud Firestore with enterprise-grade encryption and access controls. You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer.
