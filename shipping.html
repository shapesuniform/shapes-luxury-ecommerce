Shipping & Delivery Policy

Complimentary Insured Domestic Shipping
15 – 22 Working Days Delivery

1. Dispatch Timeline & Production

As each garment is bespoke and handcrafted by artisanal tailors, our standard delivery timeline is 15 to 22 working days from the date of confirmed online payment.

2. Shipping Charges

We provide 100% Complimentary Insured Courier Shipping across all pin codes in India. There are zero hidden shipping or handling fees at checkout.

3. Luxury Packaging

Every creation is carefully placed in our signature rigid archival presentation box with satin dust covers to ensure pristine transit condition.

4. Real-Time Order Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive an active AWB tracking number via SMS and email. You can also track your live shipment progress anytime on our Order Tracking Portal using your Order ID (e.g. #SH-0001).

5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Policy

Because all garments are bespoke and made-to-order, Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available. All orders must be prepaid securely via Razorpay.
