Boutique Assistance

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We offer standard sizes from XS to XXL, as well as custom fitting consultations at our Chembur boutique. All measurements are in inches — refer to the size guide inside any product for details.

We craft our sets exclusively with high-grade modal silks, organic slub linen, pure cottons, and handblock printed modal. All fabrics are ethically sourced from verified Indian textile mills.

All bespoke luxury sets are meticulously handcrafted to order at our Chembur workshop. Delivery takes 15 to 22 days with complimentary insured express courier delivery across India.

We offer exchanges within 7 days of delivery for size issues. Custom/made-to-measure orders are non-refundable. Please contact us on WhatsApp (+91 98333 92756) for assistance.

Modal silk: Dry clean or gentle hand wash in cold water. Linen & cotton: Machine wash on delicate cycle. Do not tumble dry. Iron on low heat while slightly damp for best results.
