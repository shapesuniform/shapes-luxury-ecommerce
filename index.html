FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about bespoke designer co-ord sets, tailored fits, fabric care, and visiting Shapes Boutique in Chembur, Mumbai.

Shapes Boutique is located at Shop No 4, Bus Stop, Ghatla Village Rd, Near Maitri Park, Basant Garden, Chembur, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400071, India. Our flagship boutique is open Monday through Sunday from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST for walk-in shopping and private styling appointments.

Shapes Boutique (Shapes By Satiinder Kaur) specializes in designer 2-piece co-ord sets, pure modal silk floral printed sets, breathable organic linen sets, festive raw silk sets with hand embroidery, and contemporary Indian luxury pret starting from approximately ₹7,000.

We offer standard sizes from XS to XXL with detailed size charts. We also provide custom size adjustments (tunic length, sleeve length, trouser inseam, and waistband comfort elastic) tailored to your exact measurements.

Since each garment is individually tailored and finished to order, standard crafting and delivery takes 15 to 22 working days. We provide complimentary insured express courier delivery across India and express worldwide shipping with real-time tracking.

You can visit us directly at our Chembur showroom or contact us via telephone / WhatsApp at +91 98333 92756 or email at shapesuniform@gmail.com.
