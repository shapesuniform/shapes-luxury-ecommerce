Our Legacy

CRAFTED FOR ROYALS

Every creation at Shapes By Satiinder Kaur begins as a dream of fabric and thread. Our master craftsmen revive age-old techniques of zardozi, badla, and beadwork from royal households, fusing them seamlessly with structural silhouettes that contour the modern form.

We celebrate heritage that refuses to remain in the past, transforming ancient handlooms into breathtaking contemporary treasures.
